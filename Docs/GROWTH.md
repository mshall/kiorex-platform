## SQL functions (SECURITY DEFINER, both write an `AuditLog` row)
- `growth_erase_subject(sub)`: deletes the subject's touches and the visitor keys linked to it with their funnel events; touches that a decision cites as commission evidence are anonymised (identifiers nulled) because the evidence outlives the person. Audit stores `sha256(sub)`.
- `growth_purge_expired(now, linkedMonths, unlinkedMonths)`: blanks IP hashes older than 24 h, deletes unlinked touches older than 13 months and decision-cited touches older than 25 months (parameters come from `ATTRIBUTION_POLICY`), old events and fraud signals.

- Known limit: the append-only guard protects against application bugs, not against a database role that sets the `kx.growth_maintenance` marker itself; production roles should keep the app role off superuser/owner rights where possible (NEEDS_HUMAN with the AWS role design).

## Booking integration (Wave 3, GRW-P1-C)
- **Flow.** `POST /bookings` (hold) accepts the optional signed provider-link token `lt` and reads the visitor key from the `X-KX-Visitor` header or the `kxv` cookie (only a well-formed key, consent re-checked by the attribution service) and `X-KX-Platform` (`web|ios|android|whatsapp|widget`). After the hold succeeds the decision is written once per (booking, policy version) and `BookingIntent.acquisitionOrigin` and `attributionDecisionId` are set, BEFORE any Clinic booking call. `POST /bookings/:id/confirm` reuses it (a retry, a second confirm or a reschedule never decides again; a cancel keeps it) and sends the contracts 1.10.0 `acquisition` block next to the unchanged `attribution: {source}` and the verified `lt` on `POST /marketplace/v1/bookings` (covered by the request HMAC; the evidence hash is the stored inputs hash). Guest visitors have no booking identity: the pre-login visitor key and the token carry the evidence, the booking itself needs sign-in as before.
- **Flag OFF** (`growth.attribution`): no decision, no block and no `lt`: the request to the Clinic is identical to before the engine. A decision failure never fails a booking (logged, booking continues unattributed).
- **Tokens.** Kiorex verifies both formats: the Clinic's provider-link token (`<b64 {o: org, c: code, t, v:1}>.<HMAC "kx-lt1\n"+payload>`, 72 h, secret `LINK_TOUCH_SIGNING_SECRET` else `KIORLY_WEBHOOK_SECRET`) and the in-session `kxl1.` token (`GROWTH_LINK_TOKEN_SECRET`, 30 min). A forged, edited, expired, other-organisation or wrong-secret token is ignored (never an error) and never travels to the Clinic. Provisioning: `GROWTH_LINK_TOKEN_SECRET` (>= 16 chars) from Secrets Manager `/kiorex-platform/<env>/growth-link-token` (OpenTofu `growth_link_token_secret` creates the EMPTY container, `growth_link_token_secret_inject` wires it after a human wrote the value); a deterministic DEV key is used only outside production, and in production an unset secret makes every `kxl1.` token invalid.
- **Link code to organisation.** `LinkRegistryService` maps a code to ONE organisation in `GrowthAcquisitionSource(PROVIDER_LINK)`. It learns the pair from a Clinic-signed `lt` (the signature proves the Clinic issued it) or from the `ReferralLinkCreated` event when the Clinic sends it with an organisation reference. A later claim of the same code for another organisation is a conflict and is never honoured.
- **Entry points.** Web (checkout hook sends the tab's `lt`, captured from the landing URL into sessionStorage), mobile and WhatsApp (channel `WHATSAPP`, platform `whatsapp`), widget and `CLINIC_LINK` channel (explicit provider channel), waitlist offers, family bookings (decided like any booking; the dependant never enters growth tables).
- **Growth stream facts** (server, idempotent per booking, same fact twice is one row): `SlotSelected` and `BookingInitiated` at the hold, `BookingConfirmed` when the booking becomes CONFIRMED, `BookingAttended` (props `bucket: new|returning`, from the Clinic's `isNewPatientAtBooking` on the status event) at CHECKED_IN or COMPLETED, `BookingCancelled` (props `reason: patient|clinic|other`). `PatientRegistered` (keyed by `subRefOf`, erased with the person) and `WhatsAppConversationStarted` (one per new conversation, no phone).
- **Evidence for providers.** `GET /growth/v1/evidence/:bookingId` (signed Clinic request, own organisation only, unchanged) and the new `provider-analytics` reports below.
- **Clinic change (tiny, additive, reported).** `messaging/appointment-outbox.service.ts` now also puts `isNewPatientAtBooking` (boolean) on appointment status events: the 1.10.0 contract already allows it, the Clinic did not send it, and without it Kiorex cannot count attended NEW patients (provider-led ones have no charge to infer it from).
- **Contract note.** `lt` is a Clinic-side body field that is not in the OpenAPI contract yet (a contracts change is needed; nothing breaks meanwhile because the Clinic reads it as an extra body field).

## Growth analytics v1 (Wave 3, GRW-P1-J)
- **Pipeline.** Canonical events (the 15 names of `GROWTH_EVENT_NAMES`) come from web (`apps/web/src/lib/growth.ts`), mobile (`apps/mobile/src/lib/growth.ts`), the backend (booking facts, `PatientRegistered`) and messaging (`WhatsAppConversationStarted`), into `GrowthEvent` with a dedupe key. Clients send nothing before the visitor decided; an accept adds `X-KX-Consent: analytics`, a reject sends the same events anonymously; props are a closed list; specialty and provider ids cannot be expressed. `GrowthRollupService.rollup` (hourly job when jobs are enabled, `POST /ops/growth-analytics/rollup` for super admins) recomputes the last days into `GrowthMetricDaily` in one transaction (idempotent). Dimensions: market, city (governorate), district, specialty, provider (organisation), doctor, branch, campaign, channel, source kind, platform, product, care type, origin, day. Visitor-stream rows never carry specialty, provider, doctor, branch, city or district (a test asserts it on real rows).
- **Definitions (KX-32 s04).** `kiorexRevenueMinor` = commission charges that reached CONFIRMED or BILLED, once, net of reversals of charges that had counted; PENDING is shown as `commissionPendingMinor` and is not revenue; the Clinic confirms a charge at visit completion without an event, so Kiorex confirms its fact at the booking's COMPLETED. `grossBookingValueMinor` (sum of visit fees of confirmed bookings) is a separate figure labelled `isRevenue: false`. Deposits, promotional credits, taxes and provider settlements are not in the data at all. CAC = approved spend / attended new patients (null without spend), MER = revenue / spend, ROAS per source, LTV on the fee 40 x (1 - 4 percent), verdict by LTV/CAC (3 and 1). Formulas are pure (`formulas.ts`) and tested against the KX-32 s08 worked examples.
- **k-anonymity.** `kanon.ts`: any cell of 1 to 9 is `"<10"` in every JSON, chart and CSV, money and ratios resting on it too, and one hidden cell hides its smallest sibling (differencing). Cohorts show a window only when it is closed for the whole cohort.
- **Reporting APIs.** Ops (`ops_admin`, `super_admin`): `GET /ops/growth-analytics/{overview,funnel,channels,breakdown/:dimension,cohorts,unit-economics,export/:report}`, spend `GET|POST /spend`, `POST /spend/:id/approve|reject` (maker-checker, a DB CHECK too; only APPROVED lines count). Providers: signed Clinic request with `X-Kiorly-Org-Ref` under `/growth/v1/provider-analytics/*`, own organisation as a WHERE clause, no Kiorex money (revenue, spend, CAC, MER, GBV) in the audience. The ops pages `/growth-analytics` and `/acquisition` use them.
- **Schema (migration `20261129090000_growth_analytics_v1`, additive).** `GrowthMetricDaily` gains the dimension columns and a new unique key; `GrowthRevenueFact`; `GrowthSpendEntry`.

## Campaign management (GRW-P2-A, 24 Nov 2026; flags `growth.campaigns`, `growth.campaign_approval_required`)
- **What a campaign is**: a plan (channels, total and daily budget, window, non-clinical audience, UTM tag and tracked links, landing page reference, creative references). Nothing creates, edits or spends on an external ad platform (AD-1): the module has no outbound HTTP and a repository test fails if it ever imports a client. Spend arrives as `GrowthSpendEntry` lines (maker-checker, a different person approves) and, later, read-only adapters.
- **States**: DRAFT, PENDING_APPROVAL, SCHEDULED, ACTIVE, PAUSED, COMPLETED, ARCHIVED (the table is `CAMPAIGN_TRANSITIONS` in the contracts; the guards are the pure `campaign.state.ts`). Each transition runs under a row lock, appends the history row, writes the audit row and emits `CampaignStatusChanged` in one transaction. Invalid pairs answer 409 `CAMPAIGN_INVALID_TRANSITION`.
- **Approval**: needed when the policy flag `growth.campaign_approval_required` is ON (default: every campaign), the total is above `CAMPAIGN_POLICY.approvalAboveMinor`, or a sensitive channel (WhatsApp, SMS) is used. The decider is never the requester (service check, then a CHECK). The approval covers one payload hash (budgets, currency, channels, dates, eligibility); the decider must send the hash they reviewed; an edit of those fields clears `approvedPayloadHash` and a SCHEDULED campaign that needs approval returns to DRAFT. A budget increase above 20 percent needs its own `BUDGET_INCREASE` approval; a paused (BUDGET) campaign resumes only after one.
- **Budget**: utilisation = approved spend / total budget (basis points, rounded down). Alerts at the policy thresholds fire once per campaign (atomic append, outbox `CampaignBudgetThresholdReached`); 100 percent pauses with `PAUSED(BUDGET)`; reaching the daily cap pauses with `PAUSED(GUARDRAIL)` until the next market-local midnight (Africa/Cairo for EG, DST-correct); the monthly market ceiling (pro rata by month over ACTIVE and SCHEDULED campaigns) refuses scheduling with 409 `CAMPAIGN_CEILING_EXCEEDED`. Spend lines must be positive, not in the market-local future, not before the campaign and not on a DRAFT or ARCHIVED campaign.
- **Scheduler**: `CampaignSchedulerService.runOnce` (every minute when jobs are enabled, `POST /admin/growth/campaigns/scheduler/run` for super admins): SCHEDULED to ACTIVE at `startsAt` (a window already over is archived `WINDOW_MISSED`), daily-cap resume, completion at `autoExpiresAt` (endsAt plus the policy grace), budget recompute of every ACTIVE campaign; idempotent and safe on several instances; one `GrowthJobRun` heartbeat per run; skipped for a market where `growth.campaigns` is OFF.
- **Audience**: `ELIGIBILITY_KEYS` allowlist (markets, cities, districts, platforms, patient type, registration age, lifecycle stages, care types, languages). Anything else (specialty, provider, diagnosis, phone, sub) is refused with the key named. Eligibility is evaluated for one person at the moment of a touch or a send (`isCampaignEligible`), never as a list handed to an ad platform.
- **Touch tag and reporting**: a consented touch carrying an ACTIVE campaign's `utm_campaign` is a CAMPAIGN touch whose source ref is the campaign id (an ended or paused campaign keeps the CAMPAIGN kind with an empty ref); provider links, widgets and referrals are never re-labelled; with the flag OFF nothing is tagged. The rollup carries `campaignId`; `GET /admin/growth/campaigns/:id/report` sums approved spend and the campaign metric rows (funnel, cost per booking, cost per new patient, return on spend), masks every patient-derived count under 10 and everything resting on it, and answers "not connected" for external numbers until a read-only adapter provided them.
- **Public resolve**: `GET /growth/v1/campaign/resolve?utm_campaign=` answers only for an ACTIVE campaign and returns the landing path and the campaign token (the UTM value), never a budget.
- **Roles**: every Growth role reads; only super admins write (`manageCampaigns`). Reads keep working with the flag OFF.
- **Ops pages**: Campaign Management (`/growth/campaigns`, wizard, detail with overview, approvals, spend, creatives, performance, history), Promotional Campaigns (`/growth/promotions`: the campaigns whose objective is Promotion; a promotion here never changes a patient price, rewards and clinic-sponsored promotions are separate packages) and Approvals (`/growth/approvals`).
- **SLOs (PROPOSED_DEFAULT)**: scheduler start within 90 seconds of `startsAt`; a spend approval reflected in utilisation within the same request; report of one campaign under 1.5 seconds for 366 days.

## Provider verification update requests (GRW-P2-C, KP side; contracts 1.12.0)
- A Clinic or Nurse provider asks Kiorex ops to change a verified public profile field (licence number, specialty, address, display name, photo, document). The Clinic emits `ProviderVerificationUpdateRequested` (request reference, organisation, clinic, field, evidence **count**; never the value or file names).
- `partner-onboarding/verification-update.consumer.ts` queues it as an item of the organisation's existing partner connection (inside the `human` document; **no table was added**, the KP schema is frozen in W5). Unknown organisations, unknown fields and malformed references are acknowledged and ignored, never a retried 500; a re-sent request after NEEDS_INFO re-opens the same item.
- Ops (`/admin/onboarding/verification-updates`, `OpsOnly`): list (open first), detail (the requested value and evidence references are read **live** from the Clinic through the signed `GET /marketplace/v1/provider-verification/requests/{ref}` and never stored here), decide (`APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `NEEDS_INFO`; a note of 5 or more characters is required unless approved). The decision is delivered first, as the `ProviderVerificationDecided` body, to the signed `POST /marketplace/v1/provider-verification/decisions`; only when the Clinic answers is the item closed, so an unreachable Clinic leaves it open for a retry. Both calls are audited (`partner_onboarding.verification_update_*`).
- The Clinic routes are a Clinic-side addition that contracts 1.12.1 should document (NH-042 in the Clinic's NEEDS_HUMAN). `WAITLIST_OFFER_CREATED` and `WAITLIST_OFFER_EXPIRED` from the Clinic (GC-2) are acknowledged by the webhook and change nothing: the Kiorex waitlist stays Kiorex-native and keeps reacting to `SLOTS_CHANGED`.
- Tests: `test/growth-verification-update.int.spec.ts` (contract examples validate, no value in the stored item, ops roles, NEEDS_INFO round trip, Clinic unreachable).

## Patient referral engine and rewards ledger (GRW-P2-B1 and GRW-P2-B2, wave 6, 24 Nov 2026; flags `growth.patient_referral`, `growth.rewards`, `growth.patient_credit`, `growth.refund_as_credit`, `growth.family_credit`, `growth.support_goodwill`, all launch-locked)

Founder decisions that bind this code (ADR-048 Proposed, KX-30 section 14): **two separate credit systems** (this is the PATIENT one; the provider wallet is a Clinic table in another repository, no shared row, no conversion, no import); the patient credit is **closed loop** (only Kiorex deposits and prepayments), **one way** (no top-up, no transfer, no cash-out: no such route exists and `rewards-guard.unit.spec.ts` fails if one appears), the ledger is **append-only** (database trigger), consumption is **first-expiring-first**, every lot **expires**. **COUPON mode is the default**; CREDIT mode needs the rules AND the launch-locked flag `growth.patient_credit` (gate evidence recorded by a human). **Refund-as-credit is a patient option, the original payment method is always the default.** Safety controls are never flags.

**Migration** `20261217090000_growth_p2_referral_rewards_engine` (wave 6 slot, additive, idempotent; no function or index of an earlier migration is replaced, so the replay chain of `growth-schema.int.spec.ts` is unchanged): `PaymentIntent.rewardMinor` and `rewardPostedMinor` (the card amount stays `amountMinor`: capture, PSP webhooks and reconciliation are untouched); `RewardLot.couponCodeSealed`, `couponRevealedAt`, `issuedBy`, `reasonCode` and a partial unique index on `couponCodeHash`; `ReferralRelationship.holdUntil`, `completedAt`, `reviewState`, `campaignId`, `claimedVia`; tables `ReferralShare`, `RewardApprovalRequest` (immutable once decided, `decidedBy <> requestedBy`), `RewardRecoverableLoss`; the funding CHECK of `RewardLot` is replaced so a `RESTORED` lot keeps the funding label of the lot it restores; new functions `reward_reserve_guard` (an expired or held lot cannot be reserved), `reward_approval_decided_guard`, `growth_erase_rewards_engine` (called by `GrowthMaintenanceService.eraseSubject` right after `growth_erase_subject`). Timestamps written by raw SQL are `timezone('UTC', now())` (the columns are naive `timestamp`).

**Referral (B1)** `src/growth/referral/`: one 8-character Crockford code per patient (`referral-code.ts`, deny list to be reviewed by a human, registered as a `REFERRAL` acquisition source so a consented landing touch can carry it); `GET /growth/v1/referral/me` (code, link, counts, caps remaining; counts only, never who), `POST .../invites` (a share is a count, no recipient), `POST .../claim` (first claim wins; every refusal is `409 REFERRAL_REJECTED {status, reason}` with the same shape; unknown, malformed and suspended codes all answer `CODE_SUSPENDED`; self referral answers `SELF_REFERRAL`; an existing patient `ALREADY_PATIENT`). Qualification is the pure `qualify()` (`referral-qualification.ts`, 16 checks, reject beats pending, PENDING never passes) run by `ReferralQualificationService.evaluate` under a row lock: on QUALIFIED the two lots are issued in the same transaction with idempotency keys derived from `ref|relationship|role|rule version` and one `ReferralQualified` is emitted; a completed visit sets `holdUntil` (`holdDays`, default 7) and the 30-minute `ReferralExpiryJob` evaluates due relationships. The booking module tells the engine through the `REFERRAL_PORT` token (`BookingGrowthService.onCompleted` and the `BookingCancelled` fact), never the other way round; a failure there never reaches a booking. Fraud (`referral-fraud.rules.ts`, pure, eight rule ids, hashes only): in enforce mode (`growth.referral_fraud_enforce`, which the catalogue ships ON like every flag; switch it OFF for shadow mode) REJECT and HOLD act, in shadow mode they are recorded as `GrowthFraudSignal` only. Reviewer API `/admin/growth/referrals` (aggregate with k >= 10, a reviewer view without contact data, `POST :id/decision`: a QUALIFY_OVERRIDE needs a SECOND super admin to repeat the call).

**Rewards (B2)** `src/growth/rewards/`: `RewardLedgerService` is the only writer (INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (idempotencyKey) DO NOTHING, lot rows locked in id order, the deferred balance trigger refuses a negative balance underneath); `RewardIssuanceService` (kill switch, verified qualifying visit S4, currency, recoverable-loss block, annual per-user cap, campaign cap, rule budget ceiling by one atomic upsert, maximum open balance S3; a cap or ceiling of 0 refuses: nothing issues until the founder sets amounts, AD-4); `RewardRedemptionService` (plan, reserve, release, redeem on capture, restore on refund); `RewardReversalService` (the unspent part is reversed, the spent part is a recoverable loss, REFUND_CREDIT and RESTORED are never reversed, value reserved for a payment in flight is never pulled away); `RewardHoldService`; `RewardExpiryJob` (hourly; also releases reservations of dead bookings and ends household membership of dependants who left); `RewardReconcileJob` (daily 03:00 UTC, RC-1 to RC-7, never corrects, writes an audit case); `RewardFinanceService`; `GoodwillService` with the pure `goodwill-limits.ts`; `RewardHouseholdService`; `rewards-query.service.ts` (patient reads, the one-time coupon reveal, the quote).
- **Coupons**: 10-character code, only a keyed hash after the one-time reveal (`POST /growth/v1/rewards/:lotId/reveal`; until then the code is AES-GCM sealed in `couponCodeSealed`), single use (the unspent rest of a coupon expires with its redemption), bound to ONE patient (another account entering it gets the answer of a wrong code and an `RF_REDEEM_ABUSE` signal), applied only on request (a code or one tap on the voucher), never automatically.
- **Credit lots** apply automatically at the deposit checkout while `growth.patient_credit` is effective: scoped lots first, then first-expiring, then lot id (the shared vectors of `credit-ledger-vectors.json` pass; a copy lives in `src/growth/rewards/fixtures/` with a parity test against the sibling repository). The card always keeps at least one minor unit.
- **Pay integration** (`pay/`, the only edits outside the folders): `PayService.createDepositIntentWithRewards` reserves credit (key per booking and cycle) and charges the card for the rest; `capturePayment` turns the reservation into REDEEM entries **in the capture transaction** (key per payment). The Clinic is told the GROSS deposit with `tender {cardMinor, patientCreditMinor}` (no patient identity). `REWARD_RULES.postToPayLedger=false` (default): the Pay ledger holds only the card money. The patient's own refund credit is ALWAYS posted into HELD (it is the patient's money already inside Pay), promotional credit only when `postToPayLedger=true` (account `PROMO_FUNDED:rewards`, Kiorex's marketing cost, debit-normal). Release, payout, hold-for-review and the review queue use `heldMinor(p) = card - refunded + posted credit`. **Finance must decide `postToPayLedger` before the first redemption in production: with false the clinic payout is short by the promotional part** (NEEDS_HUMAN 11.33).
- **Refund destination** (`pay/refund-destination.*`, `refund-allocation.ts`): `POST /pay/v1/refunds/preview` lists "original payment method" first and as the default, and "keep as credit" second, never the default, only while `growth.patient_credit`, `growth.refund_as_credit` and `credit.refundAsCredit.enabled` all say so. The cancel request carries the optional `refundDestination` (anything but `CREDIT` means ORIGINAL; `CREDIT` while the option is off is `422 REFUND_CREDIT_NOT_AVAILABLE`). Only a refund the patient started can carry the choice; provider cancellations, expired holds, support and dispute refunds go to the original method at once. `RefundDestinationChoice` is immutable. A CREDIT refund settles as `Refund.status = CREDITED`: `REFUND_PAYABLE -> PATIENT_REFUND_CREDIT` in the Pay ledger, one `REFUND_CREDIT` lot (funding `PATIENT_REFUND`, expiry 12 months), no PSP call. A PSP failure never becomes credit (the refund stays REQUESTED for ops); if credit can no longer be issued when the refund settles it goes to the original method. On expiry the unspent rest returns to the original method (`pay.refund_credit_expired`, a system reversal, never a patient cash-out).
- **Credit-paid deposits that are refunded** restore the credit as NEW `RESTORED` lots (the ledger has no un-redeem entry; same scope, funding label and mode, expiry = the later of the original and now + `restoredLotMinDays`, never counted against caps), cumulatively: round(card refunded / card x credit), exact on the last refund.
- **Household** (`growth.family_credit`): the owner enables dependants one by one (`dep:<dependantId>` members); a booking for a dependant draws from the owner's credit only then and only while the dependant is under the guardian link; with the flag off a dependant never draws credit.
- **Goodwill and manual issue**: closed reasons, a booking or ticket reference, per-reason maximum, per-agent daily cap, per-patient 90-day cap, no grant to the agent's own or a related account, a second approver above the threshold (a support lead or super admin, never the requester), audit rows, `GoodwillGranted` event. Not built: charging the provider for provider-fault goodwill.
- **Events** (contract names, payload without any person): `ReferralCodeIssued`, `ReferralRelationshipCreated`, `ReferralQualified`, `ReferralRejected`, `RewardIssued`, `RewardRedeemed`, `RewardReversed`, `RewardExpired`, `RewardHeld`, `RewardRestored`, `GoodwillGranted`, `RefundDestinationChosen`. No consumer yet (lifecycle journeys read them in P2-E): a no-op handler keeps the outbox clean.

**Endpoints** (patient): `GET /growth/v1/referral/me`, `POST .../invites`, `POST .../claim`, `GET .../referral/rewards`, `POST /growth/v1/rewards/quote`, `POST /growth/v1/rewards/:lotId/reveal`, `GET /growth/v1/credit`, `GET|POST|DELETE /growth/v1/credit/household`, `POST /pay/v1/refunds/preview`, `POST /bookings/:id/deposit-checkout {couponCode?, lotId?}`, `POST /bookings/:id/cancel {refundDestination?}`. Ops: `/admin/growth/referrals` (+ `:id`, `:id/decision`), `/admin/growth/rewards` (+ `lots`, `lots/:id/ledger`, `manual-issue`, `lots/:id/hold|release|reverse`, `approvals`, `approvals/:id/decide`, `finance`, `finance.csv`, `housekeeping/run`), `/admin/growth/credit/lots`, `credit/goodwill`, `credit/reconcile`, `credit/reconcile/run`.

**Pricing note (AD-4)**: all reward amounts, caps and the rule budget ship as 0 and issue nothing until the founder sets them; with `DEPOSIT_DEFAULT_MINOR=0` and no Kiorex fee the ops overview shows the banner `NO_DEPOSIT_CONFIGURED` (rewards have nothing to discount).

**Honest gaps** (also in the work packages doc, "W6 A1 as-built"): the Clinic endpoint `GET /growth/staff-phone-match` (Clinic repository) is not built, so the staff-phone half of `RF_PROVIDER_COLLUSION` runs against a mock port; the PSP adapters expose no payment fingerprint and the Clinic sends no check-in or visit-duration times, so the payment-fingerprint signals of `RF_SELF` and `RF_FARM` and the short-visit rule have no live feed (all are unit-tested); sponsored discounts and the held-deposit line (P2-S) are not in the checkout lines; referral and reward screens (web, mobile, ops pages), the invite landing page and the `/invite/*` Universal Link are GRW-P2-B3; chargebacks have no PSP webhook, so a chargeback hold is an ops action; the contract OpenAPI 1.1.0 lists the old lot kinds for `GET /growth/v1/referral/rewards` (request for 1.12.1).


## Patient lifecycle CRM (GRW-P2-E, wave 6, 24 Nov 2026; flags `growth.lifecycle_journeys` ON, `growth.marketing_messaging` launch-locked; messaging stays MOCKED)

Code: `apps/api/src/growth/lifecycle/` and `src/engagement/`. It sits ON the existing consent and preference centre (GRW-P1-I), the suppression list and the notification layer; it adds the four message classes, the stage model, eight journeys and one send gate that explains every decision.
- **Classes (AD-6)**: exactly `TRANSACTIONAL`, `SECURITY`, `CLINICAL`, `MARKETING`. Every template carries one class (`messageClassOf`, `templateClassView`); the notify purpose `REMINDER` maps to TRANSACTIONAL with the REMINDERS consent; a MARKETING template is bound to the MARKETING purpose and can never ride another; a non-marketing template cannot carry `promotional`. New kinds live in `engagement/templates.growth.ts` (AR in an Egyptian register, no clinical word, no emoji, Meta ids `TODO_NEEDS_HUMAN_*`, `copyReview: PENDING` on every row).
- **Stages** (`lifecycle.stage.ts`, pure): REGISTERED, ACTIVATED, BOOKED, ATTENDED, RETAINED (2 attended in 12 months), DORMANT (180 days idle, none upcoming), CHURNED (365). Facts are timestamps and counts only; `PatientLifecycle` holds no specialty, provider or diagnosis. Refreshed by the trigger scan and a nightly keyset reconcile (`LifecycleJob`, one `GrowthJobRun` heartbeat per tick). The funnel shows `<10` for a stage under 10 people.
- **Segments** (`lifecycle-segments.ts`): allowlist = the LIFECYCLE_POLICY enum (stage, platform, language, city, district, registeredDaysAgo, lastBookingDaysAgo, hasDependants, referralStatus). A specialty, doctor, reason, Rx, lab or pharmacy key is rejected at runtime and by the type `StrictSegment`. A segment is evaluated for ONE person at send time, never a list.
- **Journeys** (code-defined, `journeys/*.journey.ts`): welcome, booking-recovery (+45 min, REMINDERS consent, stops on booking), reminders (day-before deposit follow-up; the 24 h and 2 h reminders keep their path and are recorded as decisions), post-visit (one reminder after 48 h, stops on a review), reengagement (MARKETING, DORMANT, two steps 30 days apart, at most 2 per rolling 90 days, holdout slice), referral-invite and family-onboarding (an in-app card with no consent needed, then a marketing nudge at 3 and 7 days), reward-notice (issued, redeemed, reversed, one plain expiry notice). One enrolment per (person, journey, anchor); the anchor is stored in the journey key (`journey-anchor.ts`) because `LifecycleEnrollment` has no anchor column. Other modules call `LifecycleService.signal()` (rewards, referral, registration).
- **Send gate** (`send-gate.ts` pure, `send-gate.service.ts`): order journey enabled, marketing flag, holdout, consent, channel opt-out, suppression, quiet hours (defers to the end of the window, never drops), frequency cap, WhatsApp window and approved template, address, PHI guard, duplicate. One reason code per decision, written to the append-only `LifecycleMessageDecision`. Caps count distinct logical MARKETING sends of campaigns and journeys together (`EngagementService.marketingSentSince`) and take the stricter of the policy and the preference-centre defaults. Quiet hours use `quietUntil(at, quiet, zone)` of contracts 1.12.0 with the zone (the notify layer now passes `zone` too), so the Cairo offset-change nights end the window at 09:00 local (G-5; vectors for 2026 and 2027 in `quiet-hours.unit.spec.ts`). With `growth.marketing_messaging` OFF no MARKETING message is created, asserted across every journey.
- **STOP and withdrawal**: nothing is cached; a suppression row (the STOP handler, bounces, complaints, manual) or a withdrawn marketing consent is seen by the very next evaluation, which is inside the 60 second SLO by construction (test measures it).
- **Console** (`/admin/growth/lifecycle*`, ops pages `apps/ops/src/pages/growth/lifecycle/`): journeys with class badges, the policy in force (changes only through the LIFECYCLE_POLICY maker-checker), funnel, template catalogue with AR and EN previews and copy review state, send-gate counts by reason, suppression list (hash tail only), manual add (super admin, written note), lift = request plus a different second person with evidence, mock-mode test send to `LIFECYCLE_TEST_SUBS`, audited lookup by subject for the DPO. Patient side: `GET /me/preferences/classes` and a per-class explanation on the web preference centre and the app screen (AR and EN); the quiet hours, caps and "stop all marketing" were already there.
- **Tests**: unit (`send-gate.unit.spec.ts` 1000+ vectors against an independent oracle plus order, invariants and DST nights; stage, segment allowlist, journeys and template copy, caps and holdout, anchors, marketing binding), integration `test/growth-lifecycle.int.spec.ts` (31: each journey from event to Message in mock mode, idempotency, caps, quiet hours on both DST nights, STOP, withdrawal, holdout, flag OFF, erasure, console and roles, patient view), ops vitest `growth-lifecycle.test.tsx`, web and app preference tests.
- **Contracts needs for 1.12.1** (do not publish from here): `LIFECYCLE_POLICY` has no place for the journey parameters (delays, dormant and churn days, the re-engagement budget): they are code constants and only `enabled`, `maxSteps` and `holdoutBps` come from the policy; a `LifecycleDecisionRecorded` outbox event is declared in 1.12.0 but NOT emitted yet; the policy key is `holdoutBps` (the work package says `holdoutBp`).
- **Honest gaps**: (1) no per-journey parameter editor (constants); (2) `LifecycleDecisionRecorded` is not emitted; (3) the 24 h and 2 h reminder decisions are recorded by a helper that the booking flow does not call yet (`JourneyRunnerService.recordLegacyReminder`); (4) the WhatsApp 24 h window cannot be true yet (the platform keeps no inbound timestamp), so WhatsApp always goes as an approved template; (5) email is a channel in the gate but no journey prefers it except re-engagement; (6) the old `consent-gate.ts` (GRW-P1-I) still has the fixed-offset quiet end and is used only by its own tests; (7) a Playwright run of the patient web preference centre was not added (component tests only).

## Product ingest and decision for Pharmacy, Lab and Nurse (GRW-P4-B2, wave 7, 25 Nov 2026; flag `growth.attribution`; commission link INERT)

**What it does.** Every pharmacy order and lab booking Kiorex creates is decided once, after it exists, with the same pure `decide()` as a clinic booking. The decision is the append-only `GrowthProductDecision(product, factKind ORDER|LAB_BOOKING|CARE_PLAN_BOOKING, factId, origin, reasonCode, policyVersionId, inputsHash, evidenceTouchIds, parentDecisionId)` (migration `20270107094000_growth_p3_product_ingest`, written by the wave 7 schema owner), unique per `(factKind, factId, policyVersionId)`; update and delete are refused by the database. The commission link of products stays **inert** (AD-13): the origin is recorded and sent to the partner and no charge exists.

**Where (`apps/api/src/growth/products/`).** `product-decision.ts` (pure: product windows `ATTRIBUTION_POLICY.products.<PRODUCT>.windows`, values outside 1..365 days ignored, absent = the clinic windows), `product-decision.service.ts` (`GrowthProductBookingService`: `decide`, `acquisitionBlock`, `repair`, `summary`), `product-decision.job.ts` (repair every 15 minutes, jobs enabled only), `product-decision-admin.controller.ts` (`GET /ops/growth/product-decisions?days=`, ops roles, counts per product and origin, k-anonymity with secondary suppression). The pharmacy and lab services reach it only through the `PRODUCT_GROWTH` port of `growth-ports-public.ts` (optional injection: without it nothing changes).

**Rules.**
1. Evidence is a provider touch whose code is a link **of this product and this organisation** (`GrowthAcquisitionSource.product` and `ownerOrgRef`); a Clinic link, another product's link or another organisation's link is never evidence. Revoked or paused codes and touches before a consent withdrawal are dropped (the clinic's `touchesFor`).
2. A decision never blocks the order or booking: a failure is logged at ERROR with the key `growth.product.decision_failed`, counted (`growthFailureCount`), and repaired (`submit` decides lazily; the repair job decides anything older). A flag-check error is a failure, not "flag OFF".
3. Flag `growth.attribution` OFF: no decision, no block, the partner call has no `acquisition` key (byte-identical to before).
4. The partner call carries the optional `acquisition` block (contracts 1.12.0 `ProductAcquisitionBlock`: origin, decisionId, policyVersion, reason, evidenceHash, `linkCode` only when PROVIDER_LED, `product`). It never carries a person, an order line or a test name. `CROSS_PRODUCT` (a continuity decision, ADR-056 proposed) travels as `MARKETPLACE` because the 1.12.0 enum has three origins.
5. Continuity: `decide({continuityParentBookingId})` links `parentDecisionId` to the clinic booking's decision when the parent is the same person's booking and the product fact has no PROVIDER_LED evidence of its own. **No UI entry point passes it yet** (the "send prescription to pharmacy" and "book the ordered lab test" actions are not built), so today no decision is CROSS_PRODUCT in practice.
6. The link registry learns the product from `ReferralLinkCreated` and `ReferralLinkRevoked` (`product`, `orgRef`); a code owned by another organisation is a CONFLICT whatever the product; a code registered first by a signed touch (product unknown, CLINIC) is relabelled by the event of the same organisation. `confirmPartner` reads the signed lookup of the owning Pharmacy or Lab registration (`GET /referral-links/{code}`).

**Partner side (copy, never import).** `kiorly-pharmacy` and `kiorly-lab` (`modules/growth/acquisition-fact.ts`, migration `20270107110000_growth_acquisition_fact`): optional nullable columns on `KiorexOrder` and `LabOrder` (`acquisitionOrigin`, `acquisitionDecisionRef`, `acquisitionReason`, `acquisitionLinkId`, two counted-at stamps), a CHECK on the origin, a parser that ignores a malformed, foreign-product or absent block, and `ProviderReferralLinkDaily.orders` (on storing the order) and `.completed` (on dispense in Pharmacy, on report release in Lab) credited **once per order** and only for a PROVIDER_LED decision whose `linkCode` is a link of that same organisation.

**Tests.** KP unit `product-decision.unit.spec.ts` (5), integration `growth-product-decision.int.spec.ts` (14: decisions, product and organisation isolation, revoked link, append-only, 12 parallel calls, policy windows, continuity, contract-valid block, flag OFF, failure never blocks and repair, ops summary with masking, registry with product, pharmacy and lab flows against the fake partners); PH `test/acquisition-fact.e2e.test.ts` (3), LB `test/acquisition-fact.e2e.test.ts` (3); workspace `e2e/test/53-growth-product-ingest.e2e.ts` (3, real stack, repeatable).

**Honest gaps.** (1) Funnel events `order_started`, `order_placed`, `lab_booking_started`, `lab_booking_placed` and the rollup dimension `product` are not recorded: the event names are a contract list (1.13.0 request); the decision rows give the counters until then. (2) `PatientAcquisitionRecorded` has no `product` yet. (3) `CARE_PLAN_BOOKING` (home care) has the table support and the pure function but no call site. (4) The Kiorex landing routes `/pharmacy` and `/lab` of the link resolver are not verified against the product pages. (5) Staff-app and web "orders from your link" numbers (P4-B3) read the partner counters, which now fill; the screens are P4-B3. (6) `ATTRIBUTION_POLICY.products` is read from the payload but the JSON Schema of 1.12.0 may refuse it as a new key (1.13.0 request), so per-product windows cannot be set through maker-checker yet.

## Cross-product journeys: clinic visit to Pharmacy, Lab and home visit (GRW-P4-A, wave 8 lane A3, 25 Nov 2026; flag `growth.cross_product`, launch-locked; commission link INERT; no reward)

**What it does.** Measurement and gentle in-app prompts only. After a clinic visit the patient may see a card in the app ("Send your prescription to a pharmacy", "Book your lab test", "Book a home visit"). If the order, booking or care-plan request that follows is made through that card (or attaches the shared prescription) and the patient agreed to the connection, the downstream decision of P4-B2 has origin `CROSS_PRODUCT`, `parentDecisionId` = the clinic decision, and an append-only `CrossProductContinuity` row records the link. Growth sends no message: the cards live in the patient's own account.

**Rules (ADR-056, proposed; pure code in `growth/cross-product/continuity-rules.ts`, rule vectors in its unit spec).** (1) A downstream fact is decided on its own evidence first. (2) Through a continuity entry point with a parent, and when it has no PROVIDER_LED evidence of its own, the origin is CROSS_PRODUCT (own PROVIDER_LED evidence still wins and the link is still recorded). (3) The link keeps `parentOrigin` (PROVIDER_LED stays visible so a future rate can exempt it); no rate, amount or charge exists in the code. (4) A downstream fact never creates a reward and never counts as a referral qualification: the folder has no import of rewards, referral, credit, pay, campaigns or lifecycle (a unit guard fails otherwise). (5) The growth tables never hold items, test names, diagnoses, specialty or clinic.

**Where (`apps/api/src/growth/cross-product/`).** `continuity-rules.ts` (pure), `continuity-refs.ts` (keyed 32-hex hash of a fact id; the key is derived from the field key with the label `cross-product:fact`), `continuity.service.ts` (`CrossProductService`: consent, entry points and cards, `resolve`, `recordLink`, purge, erasure, export), `continuity.consumer.ts` (`CrossProductConsumer` reads the type and the ids of `PRESCRIPTION_SHARED` and of a Clinic-edition `LabOrderPlaced` and nothing else, plus the daily purge job and the erasure and export contributors), `continuity-funnel.service.ts` and `cross-product.controller.ts` (patient and ops endpoints). Tables (migration `20270128092000_growth_p4_cross_product`, written by this lane inside the W8 slot reserved for the schema owner; merge with lane A4's file if both exist, the statements are idempotent): `CrossProductContinuity` (append-only by trigger and CHECKs: product pair, entry point, 32-hex references, origins, `lagHours`) and `CrossProductEntryPoint` (the patient's own prompt state: kind, keyed hash, the id of their own clinic booking, expiry, consumed, dismissed; hard purge 60 days after expiry; erased with the subject).

**Entry points and call sites.**
- Pharmacy: `PharmacyService.createOrder` passes the attached prescription record and the optional card id to the product decision. The patient's own ACTIVE prescription whose encounter hashes to a prompt (or the card id) is the entry point; the parent is the patient's attended booking at that clinic within 7 days before the share.
- Lab: `LabService.book` passes the card id (header `X-Kx-Continuity`). The prompt is created when a Clinic-edition `LabOrderPlaced` arrives; the Nurse edition is relayed by its own path and creates none.

## Experiments and A/B testing (GRW-P3-A, wave 7, 25 Nov 2026; flag `growth.experiments`, depends on `growth.analytics_visitor` which is launch-locked)

**What it does.** A super admin designs a controlled experiment on one allowed surface (landing copy, sign-up value proposition, referral prompt, post-booking share prompt, app tile order, a win-back subject line); a different super admin approves and starts it; visitors with analytics consent (or signed-in people on in-product surfaces) are assigned to a variant by a deterministic function; the hourly evaluator turns exposures and funnel facts into a result; a winner is only ever a **proposal** that a human carries out through the existing two-person flows. An experiment never changes money, commission, attribution, safety, consent, privacy, price, fee, cancellation or clinical content (AD-8, ADR-025: safety is never a flag and never an experiment).

**Where (`apps/api/src/growth/experiments/`).** `experiment.types.ts` (statuses, `EXPERIMENT_POLICY` defaults, the metric registry), `assignment.ts` (pure: `HMAC-SHA256(salt, unitKey)`, first word decides membership of the allocation, second word picks the variant by cumulative weights; stop and non-RUNNING status give control at once), `surfaces.ts` (the closed allowlist, the denylist of tags and name tokens, the flag-key check), `safety-tokens.ts` (reads `safety-never-flags.json` from the vendored contracts), `eligibility.ts`, `stats/` (`normal.ts`, `fixed-horizon.ts` two-proportion z with Newcombe and Wilson intervals and Holm, `power.ts`, `srm.ts` chi-square, `msprt.ts` mixture SPRT always-valid p-values, `guardrails.ts` non-inferiority, `evaluate.ts` the pure verdict), `experiment.service.ts` (lifecycle, history, audit, outbox events), `exposure.service.ts`, `experiment-rollup.service.ts`, `experiment-evaluator.job.ts` (hourly, heartbeat in `GrowthJobRun`), `experiments-admin.controller.ts` (`/admin/growth/experiments`), `experiments-public.controller.ts` (`/growth/v1/experiments/...`), `ssr-variant.ts` (the `kxe` cookie). Web `apps/web/src/lib/experiments.ts`; ops `apps/ops/src/pages/growth/experiments/` (one route, views in the query string).

**Rules.**
1. **Never-experiment wall, three layers.** `surfaces.ts` is a closed allowlist (a surface not on it is `422 EXPERIMENT_SURFACE_FORBIDDEN`); names and tags are checked against a denylist and against every `forbiddenKeyTokens` entry of `safety-never-flags.json` (a unit test enumerates that file and fails if a registry entry intersects it); the database repeats the name rule as the CHECK `GrowthExperiment_surface_never_check`. A linked flag key may not be a safety, money or launch-locked key (`422 EXPERIMENT_FLAG_FORBIDDEN`). A variant carries only `key` and `weightBp`: there is no payload that could alter anything.
2. **Assignment.** Unit = the consented visitor key (`X-KX-Consent: analytics` AND the `kxv` cookie) or the signed-in `sub` (in-product surfaces only); with neither, control and nothing recorded, no cookie. The salt is per experiment (an assignment says nothing about another experiment), and **the salt never leaves the server**. Stable across sessions; across devices only through `sub`. Eligibility is market, locale, platform, new or returning, plus the allocation holdback. One RUNNING experiment per surface and market (advisory lock, so 4 parallel starts produce one).
3. **Design is frozen once started** (trigger: variants, allocation, salt, surface, metric, mode, alpha, sample plan, eligibility); terminal states are final; history and result snapshots are append-only; two-person rule (`approvedBy <> createdBy`, a CHECK, and the service); an experiment with history cannot be deleted.
4. **Metrics.** Primary and guardrails are first-party funnel facts (`GrowthEvent` names in `METRICS`). A fact counts only when it ties to the same unit key (visitor facts by `visitorKey`; booking facts through `BookingIntent.sub` and the visitor keys that sub was linked to by consented touches); unmatched facts are dropped; conversions count within 7 days of the exposure.
5. **Evaluation.** Fixed horizon: pooled two-proportion z-test, Newcombe CI, Holm over several variants, pre-registered sample from `power.ts` (`baselineRate` and `mdeAbs`). Sequential: mixture SPRT, always-valid p-value (alpha over the number of treatments). SRM chi-square below p 0.001 hides the results and stops the experiment (`ExperimentSrmDetected`). No winner while SRM, the minimum days (7), the minimum sample or the planned horizon (fixed mode) is unmet. Guardrails use non-inferiority margins with an always-valid bound and at least 20 events in the arm; a breach auto-stops. Far past the end (2 x planned days) with too little sample: stopped `END_DATE_INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE`. Flag OFF: the evaluator stops running experiments (`KILL_SWITCH`), the public fetch answers 200 with an empty list, writes answer `403 FEATURE_DISABLED`, STOP and ARCHIVE are never flag-gated.
6. **Ship winner never applies anything.** It writes a `SHIP_REQUESTED` history row, an audit row and an outbox event with the proposal (winner, flag key, next step); a human changes the flag or a config version through the existing maker-checker. (The control plane has no "turn ON" request path, only OFF, so the flag change itself is the normal Super Admin flag screen.)
7. **Privacy.** Arms under 10 people show `"<10"`; there is no breakdown by segment; `GrowthExperimentExposure.unitHash` is an HMAC under the salt (not linkable to a person without the visitor cookie) and `subRef` is `sha256(sub)` only for authenticated units. Erasure: `growth_erase_experiments(sub)` deletes those rows (called by `GrowthMaintenanceService.eraseSubject`); retention: `growth_purge_experiments` deletes exposures older than 13 months (called by `purgeExpired`); daily rollups and result snapshots are aggregates and stay.
8. **SSR and cache.** `ssr-variant.ts` implements the `kxe` cookie (`<key>:<variant>` joined by `.`, at most 3, only with analytics consent, `SameSite=Lax; Secure`): no consent means no `Set-Cookie` and no cache-key addition; a tampered value renders control. The CloudFront cache key must include `kxe` (launch guide 11.71, tofu `validate` only). **Wired (W7 exit gate):** `SsrVariantService` (`ssr-variant.service.ts`) is called by the public site handler only when BOTH the `kxv` visitor cookie (issued and cleared with analytics consent) and a `kxe` cookie are present; the variant is recomputed from the visitor key against the RUNNING experiments (a tampered or stale `kxe` renders control) and only for the two SSR slots (`landing.home.hero_copy` on the home page, `landing.provider.cta_label` on doctor and nurse pages; the surface must be on the allowlist, flagged `ssr` and pass the denylist again). Variant copy lives in code (`apps/web/src/public/experiments.ts`; a variant key without copy renders control). A variant response is cached in process under its own key, sent `Cache-Control: private, no-cache` and `Vary: Cookie`, so the shared page cache and the CDN never hold it; the client records the exposure after hydration. Tests: web `experiments-ssr.test.tsx`, API `public-site-experiments.int.spec.ts` (real SSR bundle).

**Schema (wave 7, owner lane A1).** Migrations `20270107090000_growth_p3_experiments`, `...091000_growth_p3_attribution_cohorts`, `...092000_growth_p3_fraud`, `...093000_growth_p3_seo_automation`, `...094000_growth_p3_product_ingest`, `...095000_growth_p2s_sponsored_promotions` (the `...099000_growth_p3_fixups` slot is unused). Additive and idempotent; no function or index replaced (new functions `growth_erase_experiments`, `growth_purge_experiments`), so nothing joins the replay chain of `growth-schema.int.spec.ts`. Privacy and money rules are database rules: cohort cells need k >= 10 or are stored suppressed with every measure zero, LTV rows are labelled ESTIMATE with n >= 10, fraud signals and decision logs are append-only, a sponsored booking line moves only forward and carries no key to the patient credit tables. `growth-p3-schema.int.spec.ts` (12 tests) covers triggers, CHECKs, erase and purge and replay twice. A request for a column or table goes to this owner as one line (the fixups slot).

**Endpoints.** Admin (`/admin/growth/experiments`): list, `catalog`, create, get, `history`, `results`, edit (draft), `submit`, `approve` (a different super admin), `stop` (reason), `archive`, `ship-winner`, `evaluator/run`. Public (`/growth/v1/experiments`): `assignments` and `exposures` (visitor), `me/assignments` and `me/exposures` (signed in). Capability `manageExperiments` (super admin) in `growth-access.ts`.

**Tests.** Unit (`stats.unit.spec.ts` 17: normal and chi-square reference values, Wilson, z, Holm, power 3,841 per arm, SRM, mSPRT hand value and a seeded 400-run A/A false-positive check, guardrails; `evaluate.unit.spec.ts` 8: time-travelled verdicts; `experiments.unit.spec.ts` 15 including a uniformity chi-square on 1,000,000 units and the safety-file walk), integration `growth-experiments.int.spec.ts` (25: lifecycle and wall, consent matrix, eligibility, exposure idempotency, 600-visitor split, evaluation to WINNER, SRM, guardrail breach, end date, flag OFF, erasure and purge, no salt in any response), `growth-p3-schema.int.spec.ts` (12), web `experiments.test.ts` (4), ops vitest `growth-experiments.test.tsx` (10, EN and AR, axe), Playwright `growth-experiments.spec.ts` on the real stack (7, AR RTL and EN, light and dark, 320 to 1440 px, axe with contrast, 44 px targets, keyboard-only, reduced motion), e2e suite 34 (real stack, two real super admins, backdated evaluation).

## Fraud detection v2 (GRW-P3-D, wave 7, 25 Nov 2026; flag `growth.fraud_scoring` arrives with contracts 1.13.0 and until then an unknown flag evaluates ON; config kind `FRAUD_RULES`; SHADOW by default)

**What it is.** Eleven scored rules (`r_self`, `r_dup`, `r_bot`, `r_farm`, `r_fakebooking`, `r_collusion`, `r_cancel`, `r_redeem`, `r_touch_burst`, `r_widget_abuse`, `r_lt_replay`) in `apps/api/src/growth/fraud/rules/`. The eight referral rules wrap the P2-B1 detection logic (`evaluateReferralRule`, thresholds unchanged); the touch, widget and link-token rules are new; the old `touch-rules.ts` stays the recorder and now also feeds the scorer. A rule returns a strength 0..1 and a reason; `scoring.ts` turns signals into a score: each contribution is `min(cap, weight x strength)`, sorted from the strongest, the i-th counts `diminishing^i` of itself (default 0.6), clipped 0..100. Bands: below 30 ALLOW, 30 to 69 REVIEW, 70 and above HOLD (stored as `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH` in `FraudCase.band`).

**Shadow and enforce.** `FRAUD_RULES.enforce=false` is the shipped default and every rule's own `enforce` is false: scores, cases and the "would have held" decision are recorded, nothing is held, rejected, suspended, throttled or reversed. A rule acts only when the master switch AND its own switch are on (maker-checker, two people). The action follows the score of the ENFORCED rules alone; the shadow score of all rules is stored next to it, so the shadow report can show precision and volume before a rule is flipped.

**What an enforced action can do (closed list of eight verbs).** `OPEN_CASE`, `REJECT_CLAIM`, `HOLD_REWARD` (P2-B2 `RewardHoldService`), `SUSPEND_REFERRAL` (the referrer's invite code goes `SUSPENDED`), `THROTTLE_ORIGIN` (a marker in the shared limiter; the widget guard answers 429), `QUARANTINE_TOUCH` (an audit row), `OPEN_PROVIDER_REVIEW`, `NONE`. There is no verb for an attribution decision, a commission, a booking or a provider's standing (guard test `fraud-guard.unit.spec.ts` greps the imports and the writes). A provider-side finding (`r_collusion`) is a `PROVIDER` case that only ops act on through the existing moderation; disputes stay in the Clinic dispute workflow. Legit families and receptionists: a declared household sharing a phone and a booking made on behalf of a patient lower the strength of `r_self`, `r_dup` and `r_collusion` below the review band.

**Cases.** Tables from migration `20270107092000_growth_p3_fraud` (owner A1). One open case per (market, subject hash, kind); new signals add to it and the score is recomputed from the stored weights. `FraudCaseSignal` and `FraudDecisionLog` are append-only by trigger; a decided case never goes back to open (trigger). Evidence is hashed references and the P2-B1 check rows only. The identity lookup (`POST /admin/growth/fraud/cases/:id/identity`) needs the `dpo` role (a super admin alone gets 403), needs a written reason and writes an audit row.

**Review.** `GET /admin/growth/fraud/cases` (filters, SLA breach flag), `cases/summary`, `cases/:id`, `assign`, `decision` (`CONFIRM` or `DISMISS`), `bulk-dismiss` (low and medium only, one reason of at least 10 characters), `appeal`, `appeal-decision`, `rules`, `shadow-report`, `fairness`, `jobs/run`, `calibration/propose`. Dismissal needs a reason and releases any hold and suspension the case caused. A confirmation that REVERSES reward value moves money: the first call records the request (audit `growth.fraud.confirm_requested`), a DIFFERENT reviewer's call executes it with the requested parameters (`growth.fraud.confirm_executed`); the config flag `twoPersonOnMoney` cannot be switched off. A confirmation without a reversal (suspension only) is one reviewer. Reversal goes through `RewardReversalService` (a spent part becomes a recoverable loss; refund credit is never reversed). A suspension of N days (at most `banMaxDays`) ends by itself in the hourly job. An appeal is filed on a CONFIRMED case inside `appealWindowDays` and decided by a different reviewer; an upheld appeal re-enables the code, and reversed reward value is NOT restored by code (a human goodwill grant does that).

**Jobs (hourly, idempotent).** SLA sweep (one audit row per breached case), housekeeping (decided cases past the appeal window close; ended suspensions reinstate), calibration counts per rule and day (`FraudCalibration`), and once a day a DRAFT weight proposal: precision at least 0.9 raises a weight 10 percent, below 0.7 lowers it 10, below 0.5 lowers it 25, only for rules with 20 labelled cases, WEIGHTS ONLY (never `enforce`, bands or actions). The draft is created by `system:fraud-calibration`, so a super admin must approve it and the creator can never be the approver; while contracts do not admit the kind it is kept as an audit proposal. Weekly fairness report: hold rate by language and by governorate, groups under 10 masked, a group at twice the lowest rate flagged for a human look.

**Wiring (all optional through the `FRAUD_SCORER` token).** `ReferralFraudService.escalate` (claim and qualification: a v2 `REJECT_CLAIM` becomes `REJECT`, a v2 `HOLD_REWARD` becomes `HOLD`; v2 only ever makes the P2-B1 verdict stronger), `FraudSignalService.onTouch`, `TouchService` (link-token forging and replay), `WidgetRequestGuard` (every denial is also a scorer input; a throttled origin answers 429 `WIDGET_ORIGIN_THROTTLED`). With the module absent or the flag off nothing changes.

**Configuration.** `FRAUD_RULES`: `enforce`, `bands`, `diminishing`, `reviewSlaHours`, `holdDays`, `suspendDays`, `appealWindowDays`, `banMaxDays`, `twoPersonOnMoney`, `rules.<id>.{enabled,weight,cap,enforce}`, `actions.<surface>.{REVIEW,HOLD}` (validated against the allowed verbs per surface), `thresholds`. The reader is tolerant (`FraudConfigService`); `validateGrowthConfig` runs `validateFraudRules` for the kind. Numbers are PROPOSED_DEFAULT.

**Not built or not true yet.** Contracts 1.13.0 must add the config kind, the flag and the events before the maker-checker can store a `FRAUD_RULES` version (`assertKind`); the two-person confirmation uses the audit log because `FraudDecisionLog.action` has no proposal value (a fixups migration could add `PROPOSED` and `APPROVED`); there is no separate ban-list table (a "ban" is the suspension with an expiry); `QUARANTINE_TOUCH` is recorded (`quarantinedTouchIds`) but the analytics reports do not exclude those touches yet; a `FraudCase` keeps only a keyed hash, and an erasure request does not delete it (the table is append-only; counsel to confirm the legal basis and retention, NEEDS_HUMAN); the `r_fakebooking` short-visit signal still needs check-in and check-out times from the Clinic.

## SEO automation (GRW-P3-F, wave 7, 25 Nov 2026; flag `growth.seo_automation` arrives with contracts 1.13.0, unknown flags evaluate ON; config kind `SEO_AUTOMATION`; `autoNoindex` OFF by default)

**Pieces** (`apps/api/src/growth/seo-automation/`). `quality-gate.ts` runs nine check functions over a crawl of the pages the sitemaps list, in both locales, and returns one report per page and locale (`SeoQualityReport`: score, failed checks, content hash; a stored `~noindex` or `~omit:<locale>` entry in `failedChecks` is the gate's decision). Checks: `thin-content` (unique words per template; boilerplate shared by 60 percent of at least five pages of a template is not counted), `empty-page` (placeholder, "coming soon", no heading, no provider), `available-providers`, `similarity` (4-word shingle Jaccard and containment against the parent hub and siblings, default limit 85 percent), `parity-ar-en` (both locales complete and in the right script; only the incomplete locale becomes noindex, the other omits the alternate and `x-default` moves to it), `title-meta` (length, leaked placeholders, duplicates: warnings), `structured-data` and `review-markup` (allowed types, forbidden contact keys, required properties, review markup only when `reviewMarkupEnabled` and the visible review count meets the minimum), `canonical`, `hreflang` (self, other locale, x-default, absolute, reciprocal), `orphan` (warning), `claims` (no medical claims or unverifiable superlatives, English and Arabic phrases from the config). Blocking checks come from `SEO_AUTOMATION.blocking`; a blocking failure costs 20 points, a warning 5.

**The switch.** With `autoNoindex=false` (default) the gate only reports (ops item and `SeoQualityGateFailed` per new failure). With it `true` a failing page is `noindex` (meta and `X-Robots-Tag`), leaves the sitemap shards and receives no computed links; turning it off and re-running restores it. `RenderController` and `SitemapService` read the stored decisions through the `SEO_QUALITY` port (cached 30 s); nothing is measured inside a request.

## Reporting attribution models (GRW-P3-B, wave 7 lane A2, 25 Nov 2026; flag `growth.attribution`; reporting only)

What exists: `src/growth/attribution-models/`. Seven models (`LAST_TOUCH`, `LAST_NON_DIRECT`, `FIRST_TOUCH`, `LINEAR`, `TIME_DECAY` with a 7 day half-life, `POSITION_BASED` 40 first / 40 last / 20 shared by the middle, the **default report**, and `DATA_DRIVEN`, a first-order Markov chain with the removal effect). **Reporting only (AD-9):** the service reads touches, `BookingAttended` (new) events, booking subs and revenue facts and writes only `GrowthAttributionModelResult` and `GrowthPathSnapshot`; a test asserts the `GrowthAttributionDecision` rows are byte-identical after a run, and a reporting window never changes `ATTRIBUTION_POLICY`.
- **Exact arithmetic.** A conversion is 1,000,000 parts; every path model returns integer parts that sum to exactly one conversion, revenue is split by largest remainder, so each model's total conversions and revenue equal the facts to the minor unit (tested on 500 random paths and in the database). A conversion with no touch is credited to `UNATTRIBUTED`, so nothing is lost.
- **Windows.** 30 days for web and app touches, 7 for campaign touches, 30 for provider links (matches the money policy); counted in **market days** (Cairo): the touch on local day 30 counts, day 31 does not, whatever the hour. Defaults are code constants until the `ATTRIBUTION_MODELS` config kind is published (contracts 1.13.0 request, launch guide 11.76).
- **Cross-device.** Touches join one person only through `sub` and `visitorKey` already recorded together on a touch (`identity-link.ts` refuses any other key); a visitorKey linked to two different subs joins neither; analytics WITHDRAWAL removes earlier touches. No fingerprint, no probabilistic matching, no third-party graph.
- **Data-driven gate.** Computed only with at least 1,000 attended new patients in the last 90 days, otherwise the page says "insufficient volume" and no touch is read; nodes seen in fewer than 10 paths fold into `OTHER`. The Markov removal effect credits last-step touches heavily by construction (documented on the page).

## Cohort analytics and LTV (GRW-P3-C, wave 8 lane A2, 25 Nov 2026; flag `growth.cohorts_ltv` arrives with contracts 1.13.0, an unknown flag evaluates ON; config kind `LTV_POLICY`; reporting only)

What exists: `src/growth/cohorts/`. A rollup (daily job when jobs are enabled, `POST /admin/growth/cohorts/rollup` for a super admin) recomputes the last N months (default 24) and **replaces** them, so it is idempotent.
- **Cohorts.** Acquisition month by the **first completed booking** (the canonical `BookingAttended` fact, as the attribution models) or by **registration month** (patients who never booked count in the cohort). Months are market calendar months (zone from the market registry). Cuts stored in `GrowthCohortMonthly`: all patients, `SOURCE` (kind of the touch that decided the origin, else the origin: `PROVIDER_LED`, `MARKETPLACE`, `UNATTRIBUTED`; `REFERRAL` is a source kind, so referral status is covered), `CAMPAIGN` (the campaign id of the winning touch) and `PLATFORM` (the touch `utm_source`, safe-listed). Per cell: patients, retained patients, completed bookings, revenue (Kiorex revenue facts: confirmed or billed charges minus reversals, the same rule as the attribution models) and reward cost (platform-funded `REDEEM` ledger entries, P2-B2) per month since acquisition.
- **k = 10 is a database rule.** A cell built from fewer than 10 patients is stored suppressed with every measure zero (CHECKs from the wave 7 migration), a source cut with exactly one hidden sibling also hides the smallest visible sibling (no differencing by subtraction), and the API masks any retained count under 10 together with the bookings and money derived from it ("fewer than 10" in the console). `LTV_POLICY.minCohortPatients` and `minPatientsPerObservedMonth` cannot be set below 10.
- **LTV (AD-10).** Observed cumulative contribution (revenue minus platform-funded rewards, switchable) per acquired patient to the horizon (12 months). For the months a group is not old enough to have, a **geometric tail** from the decay of the last six observed months (clipped to [0, 1] and never above the last observed level), optionally discounted. A month is used only when at least 10 patients are old enough to have it. The interval is a **seeded bootstrap over patients** (1,000 resamples, 90 percent, seed in the policy hashed with the group key): the same inputs and seed always give the same estimate; a group above 20,000 patients is subsampled deterministically and says so. Output rows are `ESTIMATE` with n, horizon, `modelVersion = ltv-v1+<policy hash>` (a policy change creates a new version, history stays). **No per-person LTV exists** (the model's input record is folded in memory and never stored or returned) and **a BG/NBD style model is deliberately not built**: the volume is too low to fit it honestly (documented decision of the package).
- **Payback and LTV:CAC.** Blended CAC per cohort month = approved `GrowthSpendEntry` of that month over patients acquired; payback month = first month whose cumulative contribution per patient reaches the CAC; LTV:CAC carries the LTV interval (the CAC is a single figure). Without approved spend the page says so and shows no CAC.
- **Reconciliation.** `GET /reconcile` recounts acquired patients and revenue from the source facts and compares them with the stored cells (the integration test asserts equality on 200 patients).
- **Surfaces.** `GET /admin/growth/cohorts` (table, `basis`, `dimension`, `value`), `/retention` (heatmap rates), `/ltv`, `/payback`, `/values`, `/reconcile`; ops pages `growth/cohorts` (cohort table, retention heatmap with the rate printed and a bar, LTV, payback), AR and EN. Reads: every Growth ops role; recompute: super admin; flag OFF answers `403 FEATURE_DISABLED` and hides nothing stored.
- **Configuration.** `LTV_POLICY` is validated locally (`ltv-policy.ts`) and editable in Growth configuration through maker-checker (the console form uses a local JSON Schema until contracts 1.13.0 carries the kind). PROPOSED_DEFAULT: horizon 12, tail window 6, discount 0, reward cost included, 1,000 resamples, seed 20270128, interval 90 percent.
- **Tests.** Unit `cohorts/cohorts.unit.spec.ts` 25 (200 synthetic patients with hand-computed tables, Cairo month edge, k suppression and secondary suppression, registration basis, known-decay population within 3 percent with the truth inside the interval, tail clipping, floor, determinism, subsampling, payback, policy validation); integration `test/growth-cohorts.int.spec.ts` 6 (the same 200 patients as bookings, events, revenue facts, touches and decisions: table, masking, ESTIMATE rows, reconciliation, idempotency, spend, maker-checker policy, roles, flag OFF, no identifier in any response); `test/growth-w8a2-schema.int.spec.ts` 4; ops vitest and Playwright (see below).
- **Honest gaps.** (1) Language is not a cohort cut: the table has no column for it (a request to the schema owner). (2) "Repeat rate" is shown as visits per patient to month 3, because the share of patients with a second visit needs person-level data that is not stored. (3) The reward-cost path is covered by a unit vector and the rewards ledger query, not by an integration fixture with real lots. - **A separate ledger, no wallet.** The sponsor-funded benefit lives in `CorporateBenefitEntry` (append-only; types `RESERVE`, `REDEEM`, `RELEASE`, `REVERSE`). It never writes `RewardLot` or `RewardLedgerEntry` (patient credit, ADR-048) and never touches the Clinic provider credit (CR-05): the work-package text that said "uses the P2-B2 ledger with `kind=CORPORATE`" was deliberately not followed (founder brief of wave 8). `RewardLot.kind = CORPORATE` stays in the enum and is unused. There is no balance column, no top-up, transfer, gift or cash-out route (`corporate-guard.unit.spec.ts` asserts the source and the schema). The benefit covers a share of the **deposit** Kiorex collects, so a provider's income is never reduced.
- **Money rules enforced by the database.** A trigger serialises on the account row and refuses: a second reservation on a booking, any use above the account's `sponsorCeilingMinor`, any use above the member's cap, and releasing, redeeming or reversing more than exists; the table is append-only and `idempotencyKey` is unique. The service only sizes the benefit (`corporate-math.ts`: share of the deposit, per-visit maximum, member remainder, account remainder, the card always keeps one minor unit) and retries once on a lost race. Amounts are integers (minor units, at most 2,000,000,000).
- **Terms are never defaulted.** `CorporateAccount` has null ceiling, cap, per-visit maximum, cover share (basis points) and contract reference until ops enters them after a signed contract. A `CHECK` refuses `status = ACTIVE` without them, without an end date, or when the activator is the last editor of the terms (two-person rule, also in the service: `CORPORATE_SELF_APPROVAL`). Terms are frozen while ACTIVE (pause, edit, a second person activates again). The benefit expires with the account (`endsAt`).
- **Eligibility** (`corporate-eligibility.ts`, pure, matrix-tested): the account is ACTIVE and inside its dates; a code is live; one active membership per person (partial unique index); then one path: **code** (open), **HR list** (a `LIST` code plus an approved list match), or **verified e-mail domain** (needs the `email_verified` claim and a domain the account lists; free-mail domains never count). **Passport does not issue `email_verified` yet, so the domain path answers `CORPORATE_EMAIL_NOT_VERIFIED` in practice until it does.** An unknown code is `CORPORATE_CODE_INVALID` for every unknown or malformed input (no oracle), a real code can be expired or exhausted with its own message; 10 attempts per person per hour.
- **Codes** are Crockford base32 (no I, L, O, U), 10 characters by default, hashed with a keyed HMAC (label `corporate-code`); the plain code is returned **once** at creation and never listed. Redemption counts are atomic (`UPDATE ... WHERE redeemedCount < maxRedemptions`): 12 parallel redeemers of 5 places give exactly 5.
- **HR lists** (two-person): upload hashes phone numbers and e-mails (own HMAC label) in the API; the plain values are never stored or returned; a different super admin approves; rows match only while the window is open (`CORPORATE_POLICY.hrList.matchWindowDays`, default 30, counsel to confirm) and `growth_purge_corporate` deletes them (a rejected list at once). Lists are shown as counts only.
- **Privacy.** A member is `subHash` (keyed hash, never the Passport sub). No endpoint lists members, bookings, providers or specialties; the report (`GET /admin/growth/corporate/accounts/:id/report`) returns enrolment and utilisation counts and the sponsored value, **every cell masked below k >= 10** (`minAggregateK` can be raised, never lowered; `<10` in the console), with secondary suppression so one hidden cell cannot be recovered by subtraction. Free text of leads, accounts and invoice notes is refused when it looks like an e-mail address, phone number or national id (Arabic-Indic digits included). The finance statement (`/statement`, finance and super admin only) shows the month's sponsor spend, the invoice basis: it is a total, never who. Employees can leave at any time (`POST /growth/v1/corporate/leave`); history stays. Erasure: `growth_erase_corporate(hash)` anonymises the member row (called by `GrowthMaintenanceService.eraseSubject`); the ledger keeps its rows, which hold only an opaque member id and a booking id internal to the ledger (no endpoint returns it).
- **Enterprise leads** (`B2bReferralService`): `CorporateLead` plus an append-only history, pipeline `NEW, CONTACTED, QUALIFIED, PROPOSAL, WON, LOST, DUPLICATE` with allowed moves only, an owner, company-level notes. The Clinic event `CorporateLeadReferred` is ingested by the signed webhook (idempotent per referral id, a possible duplicate company is marked DUPLICATE, a payload that looks like a person is ignored). **Leads work with the flag OFF** (a lead is not a benefit); codes, HR lists, activation and every patient route are 403 `FEATURE_DISABLED` until the gate. B2B leads are not patient attribution and create no reward, credit or fee share.
- **Patient surfaces.** `GET /growth/v1/corporate/me` (my programme and my allowance usage, never a balance), `POST /join` (`{code}` or `{}` for the verified e-mail), `POST /leave`, `GET /benefit/quote?bookingId=`. Web `/corporate` and the app screen: AR and EN, RTL, 44 px targets, axe clean.
- **Port for Pay (NOT wired).** `CORPORATE_BENEFIT_PORT` (`growth-ports-public.ts`): `quote`, `reserve`, `redeem`, `release`, `reverse` keyed by booking, idempotent, never throwing into a booking. `pay/*` and the booking service were **not** edited (another lane owns them): no checkout line `CORPORATE_BENEFIT` exists yet, so today a quote can be shown and a reservation made through the port, but no deposit is actually reduced. The hourly `CorporateJob` releases holds of bookings that died and reconciles the ledger.

(2) The `CORPORATE_POLICY` contract kind, the flag and the three events are in the **unreleased** contracts set (`kiorex-contracts` branch `w8a4-corporate`); the vendored 1.12.0 tarball does not know them, so the policy is validated locally and stored values cannot be created through the maker-checker until 1.13.0 and 1.14.0 are published together (defaults are in force and listed in `corporate-config.ts`). ## Loyalty points and partnership programs (GRW-P4-D, wave 9 lane A1, 25 Nov 2026; flag `growth.loyalty`, launch-locked; config kind `LOYALTY_RULES`; migration `20270218090000_growth_p4_loyalty`)
What exists: `src/growth/loyalty/` (service, tiers, points math, config, redemption adapter, partnership offer service and code, admin and patient controllers, job, events), the migration, ops pages `apps/ops/src/pages/growth/loyalty/{LoyaltyPage,TiersPage,PartnershipsPage}.tsx` (hosted by `Page.tsx` as three tabs), patient web `/loyalty` (`apps/web/src/pages/loyalty/`) and the app screen `(tabs)/(more)/loyalty`. **Every amount, tier threshold and cap is 0 and 0 means off; the legal text of a program is a labelled placeholder; no gate is recorded.** Counsel and price gates are `NEEDS_HUMAN` (`Docs/NEEDS_HUMAN.md`, rows `LG-W9A1-1` to `LG-W9A1-9`).
- **A separate points ledger, never money.** `LoyaltyAccrualEntry` is append-only (trigger; maintenance bypass only for erasure) with five entry types: `ACCRUE`, `TIER_BONUS` (add, with an expiry), `EXPIRE`, `REVERSE`, `CONVERT` (subtract). There is no top-up, transfer, gift, sell or cash-out route, column or entry type (`loyalty-guard.unit.spec.ts` asserts the routes, the module source, the schema and the contract vocabulary). `LoyaltyAccount.pointsBalanceCache` can only be moved by the ledger trigger (`loyalty_entry_guard`, which locks the account row, refuses a negative balance with `LOYALTY_NEGATIVE_BALANCE` and refuses any direct UPDATE of the cache with `LOYALTY_CACHE_LOCKED`); 20 parallel spends of a 30 point balance by 10 give exactly three.
- **Accrual.** Only a booking that is `COMPLETED` earns (never a hold, deposit, confirmation, no-show, cancellation or a dependant's booking, which never enters growth tables), once per booking (`loyalty:accrue:<bookingId>`, 20 parallel calls give one entry), through the `LOYALTY_PORT` called by `BookingGrowthService.onCompleted` and never able to fail a booking. Points per booking and the annual cap (rolling 365 days, 0 refuses) come from `LOYALTY_RULES`.
- **Tiers.** From the number of the person's own COMPLETED bookings in the rolling window (`windowMonths`, default 12): SILVER, GOLD, PLATINUM, each with `minCompletedBookings` (0 = the tier is not defined, nobody reaches it) and a one-time bonus per year. Thresholds must rise. The tier moves on every completed booking and in the 6-hourly job as the window moves; each change writes an AuditLog row. Tier vectors are in `tiers.unit.spec.ts`.
- **FIFO expiry (12 months by default).** `loyalty_expirable_points` replays the ledger lot by lot in time order (credit opens a lot, a spend takes from the oldest lot, an EXPIRE entry closes every lot that had expired when it was written) and is proved equal to the pure model `expirablePoints` on 300 random timelines. The service runs the expiry **inside the account lock before it accrues, converts or reads a balance**, so lapsed points are never spendable and never shown as a balance (`me` subtracts them even before the job runs).
- **Conversion into a reward lot (one transaction).** `POST /growth/v1/loyalty/convert {requestKey}`: one block of `conversion.pointsRequired` points becomes one reward lot of kind `LOYALTY`, `PLATFORM_MARKETING`, issued by the existing `RewardIssuanceService` (P2-B2) **inside the same transaction** as the `CONVERT` entry (adapter `loyalty-redemption.adapter.ts`, the only file of the module that touches the reward ledger). The reward caps (annual cap per person, rule budget ceiling, maximum open balance, active lots) apply unchanged; if they refuse, **nothing is spent** (no CONVERT entry, no lot, balance unchanged, tested). A retry with the same key returns the same lot; 10 parallel conversions of a one-block balance give one lot. A database check makes a `CONVERT` name a `LOYALTY` lot of the same person. The voucher is then shown, revealed and spent in the normal rewards wallet and checkout (tested through `reserveForDeposit`).
- **Refund reversal.** `RewardsPortService.onRefundCreated` calls `LoyaltyPort.onBookingRefunded` after the referral reversal: the points that booking earned are taken back (`REVERSE`, key `loyalty:reverse:<bookingId>`, once). Points already converted cannot be taken from a balance that no longer has them: the entry records `shortfallPoints` and an audit row `growth.loyalty.reverse_shortfall` (ops sees it; the voucher is not clawed back, `LG-W9A1-6`). It works with the flag OFF (it only removes value) and never blocks a refund.
- **Partnership programs.** `PartnershipProgram` (kind `CODE_DISCOUNT`, `CO_PROMO`, `CARD_OFFER`; `fundingMode`, `budgetMinor` and `perRedemptionMinor` default 0; a placeholder `legalTextRef`), `PartnershipCode` (keyed hash under its own label, shown once, a use counter), `PartnershipCodeRedemption` (append-only, one per person per program). **Approval goes through the campaign path:** a program is linked to a `GrowthCampaign` of the same market with the `PARTNER` channel and can only be activated when that campaign is `SCHEDULED` or `ACTIVE` after its own maker-checker approval, within the campaign budget, by a **second person** (CHECK `activatedBy <> createdBy`); a redemption additionally needs the campaign to be `ACTIVE` (pausing the campaign stops redemption at once). **The budget cap is a database trigger** (`partnership_redemption_guard`: serialises on the program row, sum of redemptions plus this one at most the budget, the code limit, the window, the value, and that the lot is a PROMO lot issued for this program's campaign): 12 parallel redemptions against a budget for two give exactly two lots; the refused one leaves no lot behind (rollback). Money terms freeze after activation; ENDED is final; `PARTNER_FUNDED` cannot be activated.
- **Fraud, consent and messages.** The P3-D referral rules do not score loyalty (`LG-W9A1-8`); what applies: one redemption per person per program (unique index), a rate limit per person (10 per hour), per-code limits, the recoverable-loss block and every reward cap through the issuance service. **The module sends no message** (a static guard forbids any messaging import): a later message about a program goes through the lifecycle layer with its own consent and suppression checks.
- **Privacy.** The ops summary is aggregate only, every cell masked below k = 10 (`<10`, with secondary suppression, `suppressed` lists the masked keys); no endpoint looks a person up. The patient endpoints return own data with no booking, provider or other person. Erasure: `growth_erase_loyalty(sub, hash)` (called by `GrowthMaintenanceService.eraseSubject`) replaces the account sub by a one-way hash and anonymises redemption hashes; ledger rows stay (accounting). Free text of programs holds a partner label only (never a person).
